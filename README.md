## The owner's name — found, not used

Diane's review names him as **Pedro**. That is the only public source; it is not
on their website, their Google listing, or CSLB (which lists the business name
only). Since a customer review is a thin basis for publishing someone's name as
the business owner, the site does not use it, and Diane's quote was trimmed
accordingly.

**Ask him to confirm**, then add a short owner story — it is the one thing the
page is missing, and the "20 years" claim lands much harder attached to a person.

## What was deliberately not built

- **Their Wix testimonial was NOT reused.** It reads: *"Alexa Young, CA —
  Testimonials provide a sense of what it's like to work with you or use your
  products. Change the text and add your own."* That is Wix placeholder text,
  not a real customer. Republishing it as genuine would be a fabricated consumer
  review (the same call made on the Marcus Air build). The three real Google
  reviews replace it entirely, and they are better.
- **No owner name** (see above).
- **No "20 years licensed" claim.** The licence dates to March 2025. Their own
  site says "over 20 years of experience", which is experience in the trade, not
  years licensed. The site repeats the experience claim as theirs and states the
  licence facts separately. Both true, neither conflated.
- **No street address.** 333 Arcade Blvd is shown as "Sacramento, CA".
- **No BuildZoom score or Yelp rating.** The BuildZoom 95 / "top 22%" is a
  third-party algorithmic score, not a customer verdict, and the Yelp page could
  not be read (PerimeterX blocked it), so nothing from it is claimed.

## Design

Distinct from the other painting sites in this collection (Color Care, Cover All,
Rojas, Santana, Top Class, Mendez, VR, Rodriguez):

- Terracotta `#C4562F` + slate `#203040` + gold `#E3A552` on warm off-white —
  a paint-and-pigment palette rather than a borrowed one.
- Bricolage Grotesque (display) + Figtree (body).
- Two-column residential/commercial service split, colour-coded, because they
  genuinely do both and their current site buries the commercial list.
- Gold discount band for the senior and veteran discounts — their own offer,
  currently invisible on their Wix page.
- Eight sections: hero, stats strip, services, reviews, work gallery, areas,
  licensing, contact.
- Full dark mode, sticky header, `scroll-margin-top` anchors, reduced-motion
  respected, inline CSS, no build step.
- Sendra visit-tracking script before `</body>`.
