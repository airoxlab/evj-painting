Licensed, bonded & insured

Check the licence before you let anyone in your home.

1134654
CSLB licence number
C-33 — Painting and Decorating
Active · $25,000 bond
Verify at cslb.ca.gov →

California requires a contractor's licence for any painting job over $500 in labour and materials. Plenty of people painting Sacramento homes do not hold one — and if an uninsured painter is hurt on your property, that can become your problem.

  • Active C-33 licence — the classification specific to painting and decorating.
  • $25,000 contractor's bond with American Contractors Indemnity, on file with the state.
  • Workers' compensation insurance carried for our crew — many small outfits carry none.
  • Free, no-obligation estimates — you know the price before any work begins.
